Privacy Policy — Learn730

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Learn730 ("the app") is developed and published by Solo730 Multimedia LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Learn730.

Summary

Learn730 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no user account, no login, no server, and no backend of any kind. All app content and progress data stays entirely on your own device.

What Data We Collect

None. Learn730 does not collect any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, or any third-party SDKs that transmit data off your device.

Local Storage on Your Device

The app uses your device's local storage (localStorage) only to save your quiz scores and experience points (XP) so your progress persists between sessions. This information:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser or app data

Advertising

Learn730 contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Because Learn730 collects no personal data of any kind, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
